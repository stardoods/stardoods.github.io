Following a joint offensive from pillager and villager factions, the Stardoods Consortium was forced to retreat from Stardoods Central. The radicals were united by a shared animosity, the pillagers citing the burning of their woodland mansions and the villagers referencing their poor labour conditions and events such as the Lakia Massacre. The radicals claim this marks an "era of freedom and newfound co-operation" for two peoples previously at odds. Key Consortium member [Sebastian Grünhaut] comments 

> "I'm glad I constructed that building out of wood. It's just another mansion awaiting the torch."

All representatives of the Stardoods Consortium were able to flee to a distant frontier unharmed; this was facilitated by new age skyship technology. Previously thought lost after the sole skyship workshop - under Berta Construction - neglected to keep schematics of their work. [Miralis] of Cornflower Town was able to reverse-engineer their retired skyship, motivated by the severe back pain they had developed from carrying people several times their weight via elytra. The evacuating skyship was ditched in the ocean after an uncontrolled fire erupted from the vessel's kitchen. The incident was attributed to the high flammability of the vessel - entirely constructed of wood and fabric - lack of fire safety procedures and training, and [Berta's][Berta] inability to fry an egg unsupervised.

The Stardoods Consortium has resolved to establish itself in new territories, saying "it's prettier here anyway." Debates on how to respond to respond to villagers in the future are to be held. One movement aims to respect villagers to prevent uprisings and quell moral concerns, forgiving their treason. Another wishes to label the villagers as terrorists for associating with the pillagers (who were designated a terrorist faction in Y34). Future sittings of the Stardoods Consortium, where such debates will proceed, will be held at the hospitality of its members in the absence of an official building.
