The 8th Sitting Of The Stardoods Consortium began Sitting on the 7th Winter Y105 in WRC SYLPH-SAMA's Council Room. Present were the following members:
- [Dotor Drep]
- [Miralis]
- [Halcyon]
- [Maggelen]
- [Sebastian Grünhaut]
- [Ace Nightwing]
- [Elene Sauvageau]

 In this Sitting, 7 Proposals were made and 6 were given Sitting Assent with 1 pending redebate and revision, an unprecedented state for the Consortium. This article concerns the Staryville Villager Rights Enactment, an answer to the question on villager rights proposed after the Consortium's expulsion from Stardoods Central, and to a recent event where Sebastian Grünhaut decapitated a Starryville Villager for trespassing on his horse farm. He displayed the head near Starryville's bell with a sign under it that read "Keep your vermin off my property."

 Sebastian gave the following statement on why such extreme violence was administered.

 > This was not the first time I had found Villagers from Starryville on my property. I have escorted villagers a total of around 7 times who have been obstinant enough to return to my horse pens again and again. I went through great effort in improving the fencing of Starryville myself and the stairways leading to my horses to prevent entry and exit from the village outside of the usual means (gates). I had made this issue known to Miralis - who governs Starryville - and these villagers were not moved once, even after a year had gone and passed; so I assumed the neglect of their owner and moved them myself. I don't know how they even survived in there, do villagers eat grass? My patience wore thin on this particular occasion and I took it out on the last of this batch of villagers, an individual who absolutely refused to be moved, even under coercion, escaping to the horses with every opportunity they had unlike the more compliant ones. So I sent a message in a way it could not be ignored this time. It was received. Starryville's Villagers should not be my responsibility. However, since they naturalised themselves upon my property and became my responsibility through neglect, I treated them accordingly.

## The Proposal
Miralis, a supporter of villager rights, took to ensuring the safety of villagers universally with the following stipulations introduced through his Proposal:
1. To give all villagers proper rights as proper Citizens. 
 - Citizen is a protected Consortium title which means that villagers will be able to vote, represent themselves in a Tax or Court case, be protected by Punitive Tax and must pay any Domestic Taxes.
2. Villagers should not be harmed or killed other than if it is for their benefit or for the protection of others.
 - For example: stop them walking into danger or killing a rampaging blighted villager.
3. All villagers have worth and should be treated as such. Even the unemployed and nitwits.
4. If a villager is tresspassing it should be only removed by diplomatic means, such as by boat, gentle nudging or by ghast.
5. Villagers should have proper housing if they are to permanently live in a settlement.
6. While villages may come under Consortium territory, these are communities with rich culture. Integration is permissible, erasure is not.
 - Buildings should not be demolished unless it is to improve and act in accordance with the community. This applies to all villages.
7. Intentionally incurring a raid upon an undefended or underprepared village is a crime, they must proper defenses and armaments like walls.
8. Reparations must be given when, through your actions or inactions, you have caused the death of a villager. A stack of food must be given to at least one villager within that village.
9. If your actions or inactions cause the destruction of villager property, you must restore it as it was or improve it.
10. If a trained villager dies within Consortium-owned territory, the owner of the region must be paid additional reparations depending on the level of the villager killed. 
- Unemployed/Nitwits: 10 Asterettes
- Novices: 20 Asterettes
- Apprentices: 32 Asterettes
- Journeyman: 1 Aster
- Experts: 2 Asters
- Masters: 3 Asters

## Discussion
Discussion after the Proposal was read clarified the following:
- #10 would be enforced by keeping a record of all villagers and their training level, such as through birth registration or population censuses.
- Assaulting a villager in Consortium territory counts as assault. Outside of Consortium territory, reparations are given per #8 as Asters and Asterettes have no use there.
- Looting and stealing from a village falls under #9.
- Reparations can be paid through trade, food and building.
- Looting crops and replanting them is permissible.
- Wandering traders are villagers and their llamas are their property.
- If you are unable to pay with money, you must pay with the alternative routes to repaying reparations.
- Villagers may be put under house arrest if they are constantly trespassing.

## Opposition
Opposition to the Proposal raises the following points of contention:
- We are unable to communicate directly with villagers who process and use language in an entirely different way through "hrmmms". Thus they are physically incapable of voting, defending themselves in a Court or Tax case and paying the correct Domestic Taxes.
- It is wrong to assign variable Punitive Taxes upon the death of villagers based on their expertise. All people should be equal.
- Trading with villagers is not reparation or Tax. That is a mutualistic exchange and not a punishment for wrongdoing.
- The extra charges that condemn destruction of villager property would be additional to Terrorism charges under the P.A.P.A. Act, which villagers are protected under already (Friendly NPCs), without the title of Citizen. This orders the perpretrator to restore what was destroyed else do forced labour for the victim for 24 hours (if they are unable to restore what was destroyed) and help every member of the Consortium with a task at least once or face execution.
- #8, #9 and #10 are additional protections given to villagers that other Citizens do not have.
- #2 allows execution without due process or within acceptable provisions for execution which infringes upon the rights of Citizens.
- Paying reparations through alternate means outside of Assault and Murder charges are much more forgiving than the weight of these Punitive Taxes.

Thus, the Opposition argues they should be given an alternate title to Citizen to clear discrepancies and remove some concessions that are perceived to give them more rights over other groups within Consortium oversight.
