The Stardoods Consortium is a unitary body that claims sovereignty over all creation, a right bestowed by almighty Edward. Founded to keep peace, prosperity and order in a lawless land, it intially began as a confederation of factions - the initial members being Wulpin Rail Company, Twilight Valley, Berta Construction, The Church Of Edward and Greengrotto Grove. Eventually, factions were assimilated into the Consortium and it became the sole, supreme authority of the land. 

It meets much adversity from the Pillagers, a beligerent villager faction and ideology, who continually harass the Consortium and weak villages, even after the Consortium's ousting from their homeland. In return the Consortium labels them terrorists and delivers punishment in kind.

The Consortium was founded upon the following tenets which would adapt over time:
- Civility
- Introducing law and policy through Proposals
- Representational democracy: only members of the Consortium may submit proposals and vote "For" or "Against" them.  

This timeline does not include Proposals given Standing Assent and will be updated over time.

## 3rd Sitting of the Stardoods Consortium, Y34

1. Furina is elected as the Head Of The Church of Edward, its Matriarch
2. Tax for Murder is increased (10 silver -> 20 silver)
3. Money deducted from a person by the Consortium is defined as Tax.
- Punitive Tax: Taxes levied for illegal activity, with the victim and consortium being awarded parts of the tax (3:1), or the entire tax being awarded to the consortium if there is no victim.
- Domestic Tax: Taxes levied for legal activity (e.g. tax on alcohol, part of a corporation's profits)
4. All tax affairs are now handled in and by the tax office. Court cases are reserved for particularly heinous or non-prescribed offenses (to sue someone).
- If you wish to tax someone you can keep a record and submit it to the tax office to receive compensation from the offending party, the offending party can dispute the claim as usual
5. Pillagers are designated as a terrorist faction
- Thus it is illegal to hang their banner and associate with the faction.

## 4th Sitting of the Stardoods Consortium, Y46

1. Approval for raid on the closest pillager manor west of Central.
2. Quality tinker’s tools available for free for a limited time, subsidised by the Consortium.
3. Create a channel to Cornflower Ocean to improve travel
4. Begin minting Asters and Asterettes to replace the old currency of copper, silver and gold.
5. Death by train has no punitive basis unless intentional.
6. If a train driver causes a crash, they are taxed.

## 5th Sitting of the Stardoods Consortium, Y57

1. Elene Sauvegeau is now Tax Office incumbent de iure
2. The Tax Office incumbent is given special powers to enforce taxes (arbitrating Punitive Taxes) and make their own prescriptions for justice (within reason and according to current law)
3. Assault, 6 > 12 Asters
4. Murder is 20 Asters minimum, may be raised based on severity or history, percentage tax is applicable
5. All proposals now first exist as individual books and may be passed on unanimous vote ("Standing Vote" and "Standing Assent" respectively) or discarded on majority rejection. Non-unanimous votes will move the proposal to be discussed during Sittings. Proposals voted during this time are given Sitting Assent.
6. Proposals may electively skip the Standing Vote and be moved to Sittings.
7. Non-consortium Members can now vote!
8. Factions are assimilated into the Consortium and being a faction member is no longer mandatory for membership. There are no longer restrictions on voting: each person gets one vote.

## 7th Sitting of the Stardoods Consortium, Y96

> This is the first sitting outside of Stardoods Central as a coup took place in its original lands.

**Proposal For Improving The Architecture Of Starryville, Chasmtown and Twinkbreeze, Y96**
*aka Panstellan Division Of Labour Act*

Designate specialisations for the districts of Panstella
- Starryville: General & Food
- Twinkbreeze: Alchemy & Fishing
- Chasmtown Lower: Market
- Chasmtown Upper: Armour & Weapons

**Proposal For The Introduction Of The Consortium Gloss, Y96**
*aka The Gloss Act*
- Keep a list of protected terms defined in proposals

**Proposal For The Routine Sitting of The Stardoods Consortium, Y96**
*aka The Routine Sitting Of The Consortium Act*

- Sit on the 1st Sunday of every month @ noon UTC+0
- Routine Sitting can be amended or skipped on vote

**Protocols Against Pillagers & Associates Act, Y96**
*aka The P.A.P.A. Act*
- Griefing is a made an offense
- The criminality of displaying Pillager Symbols & Aiding And Abetting Pillagers is reinforced and given prescriptive sanctions.
- The previously stated offenses are also made terrorist acts
- Griefing is punishable by 24hrs of community servitude for each and every citizen OR execution
- Aiding And Abetting is punishable by 60% Punitive Tax AND/OR execution
- Displaying Pillager Symbols is punishable by 30% Punitive Tax
- **Confiscation** - Seizing property for Consortium ownership

**Vehicle And Machinery Organisation Act, Y96**
*aka The V.A.M.O. Act*
- Vehicles in unpermissible places will be moved or towed
- Potentially hazardous unattended machinery will be moved

**Frontier Protection Act, Y96**
- It is not permissible to exploit finite resources in private or claimed/non-public territory

**Chicken Protection Act, Y96**
- Chicken consumption is forbidden during Spring 
- Only groups appointed by the Matriarch of the Church of Edward may be subject to culling
