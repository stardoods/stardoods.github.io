## Background

The 3 villages of the Panstellan frontier have been known by the Consortium since arriving in the Starlands. Prior to a series of tragic events that befell Panstella, the villages were humble sites of trade and culture, largely undisturbed by pillager interference, and enjoyed the protection of their golems and Chasmtown blightslayers. 

Each village specialised in particular fields: 
- **Starryville**: trade & agriculture
- **Twinkbweeze Viwwage**: alchemy & fishing. The river seperating it and Starryville is ideal for aquaculture.
- **Chasmtown**: blacksmithing & blightslaying. The chasm holds much mineral value and hides a trial chamber from which great riches were exploited.

Staryville and Twinkbweeze met the "traditional" fate for villages, being consumed by a wave of aggressive blight activity across the region. Chasmtown survived the unnatural disaster but was subject to arson and pillaging after being weakened in their struggle. The Consortium initially held a non-interventionist position owed to the lack of recognition for villager rights and past conflict with the villager race. However, the Consortium eventually set on the grand and noble quest of rebuilding these areas under Consortium oversight to ensure economic stability for Panstella and its future residents. The villager population was rebuilt from immigration and curing of any blighted villagers that were quarantined after the incident, save for one cleric of Twinchurch who remained a sole survivor of the waves of calamity.

## Starryville

Starryville falls under the governance of [Miralis] who has made great advancement in re-establishing agriculture with large wheat fields that supply the frontier with bread and other cereal products. The local bread bank distributes food to those who need it and a culture has been established around the wheat that has fortified its prosperity. Flowers also hold a special place in the culture of Starryville, each having its own importance and meaning. The village is well defended with strong wooden fencing and has amenities such as a train station and a landing pad. A shipyard is planned to be constructed here in the future.

Miralis strongly supports the villager position and their welfare but has recently come into conflict with members of the Consortium who hold anti-villager sentiment and over the recent ruling to sanctify chicken, an issue that has spawned a court case set for after the proceedings of the 8th Sitting of the Stardoods Consortium.

## Twinkbweeze Viwwage

![Twinkbweeze]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/the-songbird/old-twinkbweeze.png)
<p style="text-align: center;"><i>

Under its governor, [Dotor Drep], Twinkbweeze has developed its own agriculture and a bustle of buildings, workshops, inns and restaurants - most notably Faraway Tea, renowned for its builder's tea and ethical milking (of cows) ethos. The Church Of Edward's Guardian Angel [Ji] was appointed steward of the frontier's church, a responsibility he accepted gracefully. 

Twinkbweeze's name has had a history of pinballing. Originally named Twinchurch after its church's unique architecture, Twinchurch was temporarily under the name of Amberbreeze during deliberation of claims over the frontier. Once Dotor Drep assumed governorship, he merged the two historic names to form Twinkbreeze but its corruption to Twinkbweeze was spawned from Tax Office Incumbent [Elene Sauvageau]'s mispronounciation when announcing the new name during a consortium debrief and the name stuck.  

## Chasmtown

![Wulphmaris Station at day]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/the-songbird/wulphmaris-at-day-y104.png)
<p style="text-align: center;"><i>
    Wulphmaris Station at day, Edward Jr. sits on the right.
</i></p>

[Sebastian Grünhaut] is the de facto governor of Chasmtown. Despite no current villager population, development of the region has been chugging along smoothly.

Wulphmaris Station serves the town and region at large. It is built on the grounds of a ruined nether portal and is the only portal known to be repaired and in operation in the Starlands. It possess an eastbound service ("down", towards Stardoods Encampment) and a westbound service ("up", towards the Ninth Silence). A 1 Asterette fee is levied at its toll gates to promote the development of the frontier and its marketplace. 

Trains from across the Starlands refuel here. Lava is pumped from the nether ocean and delivered by WRC Motivation, a train of a single 432 bucket capacity fluid carriage which traverses the overworld-nether boundary daily. The station also supports the arts with its gallery and museum memorialising the industrial wonder that was Edward Jr, the first ever built, who now exists in replica form. The gallery is attached to a shipyard which is planned to be able to support the large skyship WRC Sylph-sama once constructed.

Chasmtown aims to claim the title of central marketplace for itself, already hosting Elwren Hall, an inn, restaurant and the foundations of a shop selling imported goods from the Ninth Silence - all diverse enterprises from diverse individuals. The previous blacksmithing and monster slaying specialisation has fallen to the wayside with the path to the chasm's trial chamber, once the lifeblood of the community, falling into obscurity and disrepair. In fact, Elwren hall (having been airlifted to its current position from Stardoods Encampment) was unknowingly built upon one of the routes to the chamber, sealing it off completely.
