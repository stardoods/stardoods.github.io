Since the 7th Sitting of the Stardoods Consortium in Y96, chicken has been made illegal to consume in spring and slaughter outside of designated areas. Many have found themselves in deep trouble after intentionally or mistakenly eating chicken during the sacred period, even the Church of Edward's own Guardian Angel, [Ji], had to pay deep penance for contravention of this divine law. Indeed, it seems many have not yet adjusted to this righteous way of life, insofar that there have rumours of illicit chicken consumption clubs hiding in the dark, so here are some tips to help to celebrate the upcoming decennial anniversary of the Chicken Protection Act!

## 1. Eat Duck
Ducks look very similar to chickens so probably taste the same. The only problem is that ducks are not found in the Starlands, so you would need to import them from elsewhere, (like Stardoods Central). Twilight Valley was famed for its duck sanctuary deep in its basements, supposedly where the legendary duck F!ck originated from. However, importing from Twilight Valley and Central has uncertain legal and moral complications due to terrorist occupation. Stealing from the terrorists is highly encouraged however!

## 2. Go Vegan
Take the dive and cut out your carnal cravings for the succulent flesh of the living and unliving completely! Veganism has been shown to decrease risks of health problems and environmental impact. Ratatouille was once a staple in the diet of the ancient vegans and may still be reproduced to some accuracy with the ingredients available in the Starlands. Not only is there less suffering upon Edward's creatures, its also simpler. Meat must be fed with plants and can run away, skip the middleman and directly eat the plants. Simple!

## 3. Become Comatose
There is no risk of eating chicken in spring if you aren't conscious during the period of anti-chicken observance. There are many ways to become comatose: 
- throw yourself into the Ninth Silence
- stand on a railway line
- sleep in the nether
- snort lots of blaze powder
I personally recommend the last item as it is my favourite way to pass the time.

Good luck to you and your families in keeping your oaths and lives in accordance with Consortium law, I hope these tips serve you well.
