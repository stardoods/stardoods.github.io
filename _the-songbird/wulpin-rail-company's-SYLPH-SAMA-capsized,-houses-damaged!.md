During the inspection of WRC SYLPH-SAMA by SSAWA representative [Dotor Depping] and Tax Officer [Ace Nightwing] in Year 105, Villagers of Starryville were rudely awakened by sounds of wooden creaking and whirring propellers. Upon brief investigation, they found the recently constructed airship capsized; draped over the roofs of their homes.

The inspection was conducted to check the vessel for potential safety risks following a number of failed trials and in preparation for its maiden voyage scheduled during the 8th Sitting of the Stardoods Consortium. According to [Sebastian Grünhaut], the ship's lead engineer as CEO of Wulpin Rail Company, the vessel became "overstressed" and plummeted to the ground as he had failed to conduct basic flight checks. Only one of two steam engines were operational - to maintain a low-altitude hover - and upon attempting to increase power to the propellers to gain altitude, the ship's stress system seized.

Sebastian attempted to rectify the ship using a WRC manufactured plunger launcher. Dotor Depping reported the ensuing events:

> "The ship *only* scraped and crashed into a villager's house. Thankfully the roof *only* had a *tiny chunk* **torn** off." 

Eventually, the ship exited Starryville mostly unscathed and spun to Phio Shipyard or, in the words of Ace Nightwing: "washing machined us to Phio Shipyard."

Calls for Starryville Village to be compensated in bread for the damages have gone unanswered by the railway CEO who recognises no obligation to fulfil the request.
