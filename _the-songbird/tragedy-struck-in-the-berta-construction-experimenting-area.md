During testing of the Winged Vehicle Obstacle, a smaller part of the far larger and ambitious Purpose-full Adventuring Multi-Area Vehicle (PAM-AV) Project a prototype plane had violently crashed into the ground and slain a poor Villager who happened to walk into the crashing plane.<br>

The reason behind the crash was due to a lack of understanding of how a plane controlled and interacted with the physics of the world. [Berta] has expressed regret for crashing into a Villager and compensated their family with bread.<br>

[Dotor Depping] who was present during the incident commented:

> "I mean, that Villager saw it coming, it was literally flying towards them and they just stood there."
