The 8th Sitting Of The Stardoods Consortium sat on XXX. In this Sitting, 7 proposals were made and 6 were passed with 1 pending redebate and revision, an unprecedented state for the Consortium.

These are as follows:
- The Ledgers Tax, a 10% tax on all shop sales every decade. Materials to form a shop in compliance with the tax will be provided by the Consortium.
- The Sebmagstian Act, forms the institution of marriage, a union between people. Partners may file for Marital Rebate or Divorce.
- 

The following terms were added to the gloss:
**Marital Rebate** - A 1 Asterette return for every punitive or domestic tax a Partner in a Marriage incurs.
**Partner** - Another person within a Marriage who is not the subject of address.
**Divorce** - the dissolution of Marriage.

Several instances of the Consortium Courtesy Code were breached within this Sitting, including violence, threatening display and threats of harm. All of which are classified as assault under this law.

Sebastian Grunhaut had this to say

> The Consortium is still a young child at their youthful age of around 70 years. Since its very founding, we have armed it with the blades and plating of law and tax. A means to defend itself from its naivety. To defend the unity of our people. Indeed, I have been stricken many a time for my mistakes. Many mistakes for which. despite the justice and punishment I have endured, I have not been forgiven. I would know best how well this soldier fights to protect its sacred law. Why then are we allowing such softness and forgiveness to pry the defenses of our institution apart? Forgiveness? For **villagers**? Inconceivable. *Absolutely* inconceivable that figures, like Elene Sauvageau, who were faithful; sensible to our law, would entertain the idea of protecting villagers not just as property or a second class but as a vague equal. Not even equal. This proposal grants them rights *WE do not have*: in addition to retaining our protections against assault and murder, they must be paid more in resources to compensate for theft and devastation, all the while being inculpable from any kind of tax! *WE* don't have protection against theft in specific, only griefing, but they have immunity so long as they remain villagers and don't defect to the illager's militant way of life. Worst of all, it protects villagers not even under Consortium governance. I am willing to allow concession for proposals people want, I have supported proposals that would destroy my unlife's work if I made so much as a misstep because I believe in collective good, that is why I push so strongly for public service in founding the Consortium and building railways. There is no collective good in giving these creatures we have chained up, coerced and imprisoned behind fortresses of stone for their wellbeing such a thing as dignity, for they are not sapient creatures capable of lawmaking or train building. They go for a stroll at night, see blighted horrors and attempt to flee into their homes and then go right back outside again to die. You cannot call such feeble creatures our equals, they are to be kept indoors where it is safe. They are pets, they are propety, they are things. Not people, not our equals. Protect them as such. That is a narrative I would support if their protection is such a fervent desire. They panic when they hear the pealing of bells. They are bred for this so that even a villager only a milisecond old knows what that bell means. They are creatures. They are more intelligent than a cow, I will agree, but they are no more intelligent than us. They might build nests and farms and form armies but so do ants. You would not give an ant rights, you would not give these larger ants rights.
