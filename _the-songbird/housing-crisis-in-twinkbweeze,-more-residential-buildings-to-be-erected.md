As of 111, a housing crisis has been recognised within Twinkbweeze. Since then, its residents have been protesting for resources to be focused on developing and improving housing where costly projects, like Twink Castle and the newly-planned railway and station, weaken the propsect of basic living conditions for many.

[Dotor Depping], governor of Twinkbweeze, took the protests into consideration and announced in the tail of the year that the village would be constructing "apartments" to improve conditions where "sardine packing" was the norm. Depping describes the situation as the result of a "temporary" measure. Twinkbweeze needed somewhere for the initial labourers to stay so that the village could start to stand on its own two legs; this accomodation has persisted through the years despite stability being reached. Depping has been noted to characterise the crisis as stemming from "overpopulation" rather than a lack of housing.

Construction of these apartments has been slow as harsh winter arrived soon after the announcement. Work is expected to pick up with the thaw but Twinkbweeze's villagers have become increasingly restless as the situation festers. One villager that Depping nicknames "That Bastard" (whose real name is not yet known) has been more vocal about the crisis. According to Depping, That Bastard planned to assassinate him. He insisted that he was attempting to save That Bastard from an industrial accident and claims That Bastard used the opportunity to falsly report the governor's actions as abuse to the local iron golem. This caused the golem to beat Depping to the brink of death but he managed to survive. Following this incident, he released a statement:

> "I'm not exactly doing my job as a SSAWA representative and a governor if I let him get crushed by machinery. Though, I wouldn't save him next time if he ever chooses to do so again."

Conflict between Depping and That Bastard continue to escalate, with allegations of theft being flung around. Twinkbweeze's villagers are calling this a "Sign of deep dissatisfaction." with their governance. However the governor of nearby Chasmtown, [Sebastian Grünhaut], has chimed in; he regards it as "Just a storm that shall pass soon enough."
