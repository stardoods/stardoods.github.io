## About

Phio is a Skykid from the Ironsand Shores to the west; less is known about her than even [Miralis] but it is known she is the older of the 2. Her mask is made from the sands of her beach like all Skykid masks. Phio is a grownup Skykid meaning she is not only taller but is also aware of her past life something that, while unsaid, weighs on her often. Her closest friend is Miralis for several reasons, though her main one is simply due to the fact that they've spoken to each other and known the other for so long, plus she enjoys his friendliness. Phio is not a very sociable person, attending gatherings mostly due to obligation and preferring just to keep Miralis safe and work on airships. Despite her association with the Starlands she has yet to make many friends with them and was only introduced relatively recently to them (though she had heard of them from Miralis).

Phio is much more blatant in her lack of faith in Edward than Miralis, simply not speaking on the god and not going to church either. She enjoys reading books when she's alone, often enjoying novels or just short stories when she's working.
