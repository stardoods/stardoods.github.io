## About
> "I have never done anything bad in my life. Ever."

Long a presence in the Starlands and the Consortium (mostly because he founded it), Sebastian seeks to build railways to connect the world and does so for the great love of it.

Trains are all he has known and trains is all he commit to. Build, build, build. Rob you of your money so he can build. Beneath that prim dress and polite manner there is no concern for anything but trains. Or so one might think. Sebastian loves flowers! Did you know that? He should like to build gardens and sniff bouquets. Alas, his olfactory senses are redundant and his hands are bitingly cold. Indeed, he gives no warmth whatsoever. A plume of frost with every exhale. He is getting better at "caring" to some degree but it will take some time yet to thaw the permafrost at his core. He has been known to accidentally and intentionally murder people but running people over with trains is 100% never his fault. Some speak of a blaze powder habit but pay it no mind, its all just rumour, yes?

## Controversy

Sebastian has been convicted of several crimes since law and order were introduced by the Tax Office's founder, [Venessa Sunset], who did so in reaction to Sebastian pushing [Dotor Drep] off a cliff. Sebastian was taxed 10 silver (Around 4 Asters 24 Asterettes) for this act. It was later discovered Derp was killed by a pillager, and not by the fall, but the decision was final as he may have lived if he were not wounded by Sebastian. Within the same day, Sebastian  killed Derp again but this time by pogoing over his head with a bomb. He was taxed 10 silver once again. 

In the Year 21 trial, [Aral'lyre] V. Sebastian Grünhaut, the court ruled against Sebastian who was found culpable for 3 counts of murder and taxed 30 silver (Around 14 Asters 16 Asterettes). His defence was that these killings were entirely legal and consensual as Dotor Drep, the primary victim, had agreed to it via contract. The court interpreted the contract as only allowing a single killing and found the contract unfair and unenforceable with clauses ordering the signatory not to take legal action against Sebastian. In the wake of this case, more stringent and visible guidelines on contracts, requiring overt consent to killing and otherwise considering contracts that order illegal acts to be void unless permitted by the Consortium.

Multiple tragedies against villagers have been attributed to the CEO who has denied any wrongdoing. He has been implicated in the Lakia Village Massacre which involved the eradication of an entire village - population, buildings and all - from the Lakia District of Stardoods Central. He claims the population had all been wiped out either by blight or by walking onto train tracks, something he had protected them against by building walls and gates around them. Nearby villages unanimously found this effort to be a half-hearted attempt to cover up his true intent: to erase all trace of villager culture for industrial and railway progress. He refused to offer any compensation for causing [disruption within Starryville][villager no reparations] and was [found to have left a beheaded villager's head in the village square][villager beheading] with the note “Keep your vermin off my property.” 

A great body of evidence points to Sebastian murdering [Tabby] who was impaled on a stalactite trap hidden in their home. The motivation is assumed to be in response to Tabby enslaving [Berta] as a sign protesting this was found near the trap. Tabby had been accused of embezzeling public resources at the time and the murder could be interpreted as vigilantism. As of Year 111, no legal action has been taken against either Sebastian or Tabby.
