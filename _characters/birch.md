## About

Birch was hired by [Thallium] on behalf of the Consortium to head south of Edwardium to discover some of the secrets of the unstable Southland. He would lead an expeditionary force and set up a settlement there as the land's mysteries were undiscovered. At some point in time, The Expedition's letters stopped arriving, leaving the fate of The Expedition unknown. Presumably, they are still around given the contents of their recent letters. <br>

He also tried to make something he called a "Jurassic Park" once. It failed when the only thing the amber monoliths spawned were seals. What this means has been up to much speculation.
